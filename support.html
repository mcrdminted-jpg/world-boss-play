Common questions

I bought gems and they did not arrive. Open the Store tab once while online; unfinished purchases are checked and added automatically. If they still do not show, email us your player name and the Apple receipt (order id).

How do I get a refund? Purchases are made through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How many attacks do I get? Five a day, plus extra attacks from the Tech Tree, your class ability and optional rewarded ads. Attacks reset at midnight Pacific time.

How do I get gear? Every boss or lieutenant you help defeat drops one item when you claim the reward on the Home screen. Gear is on the Profile tab.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile, then hold the Delete account button. This removes your player and progress for good.

I lost my progress. Progress is tied to the app install on your device. Deleting the app or its data creates a new player. Email us and we will try to help.

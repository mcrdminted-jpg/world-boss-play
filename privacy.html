World Boss Privacy Policy

Atlas Forever Studios. Effective November 10, 2026.

The short version

World Boss does not ask for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or location. The app shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, only when you tap Watch ad. There are no forced ads and no analytics companies in the game. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What the game stores

  • A random player account. When you open the game, our server creates an anonymous account with a random ID so your progress is saved. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Gameplay data. Your damage, attacks, coins, gems, upgrades, class, tech tree ranks, badges and the bosses you helped defeat.
  • Your player name. The game picks one for you. If you change it, names are limited to letters and numbers and filtered to block contact information, social media handles and offensive words.
  • Optional country, state and ZIP code, only if you enter them in Profile. They are used for local, state and country leaderboards. Your ZIP code is never shown to other players.
  • Settings on your device (sound and vibration) are kept only on your device.

What other players can see

Your player name, badge, damage numbers and public stats. Nothing else. The game has no chat, messaging or friend system.

Who processes the data

Game data is stored with Supabase (database hosting) in the United States. We do not sell your game data or share it with anyone else.

Ads (app only)

In the iPhone and Android app you can choose to watch a rewarded video ad for a bonus (an extra attack, a 2x damage hit or a gem). The ads come from Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. On iPhone, Apple asks your permission first; if you say no, you still get ads and rewards, just not personalized ones. In the EU and UK, Google's consent form asks you first. We never send Google your player name or game data.

Children

World Boss does not knowingly collect personal information from children, does not allow free-text communication between players, and filters player names.

Deleting your data

Open Profile, Delete account and hold the button. Your player, progress and account are permanently deleted from our server right away. Shared world history (such as how much HP a boss had) stays, without your name.
